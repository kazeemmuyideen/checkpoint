Our story

Rooted in the Mediterranean, served in the heart of the city.

Saffron & Sage began with a simple idea: cook the way our grandmothers did — with what is growing, what is in season and what tastes best that week. Eight years later, that idea still shapes every plate that leaves our kitchen.

We work with small farms within a day's drive of the restaurant, press our own olive oil and bake our bread twice a day. The menu is short on purpose, and the welcome is always warm.
